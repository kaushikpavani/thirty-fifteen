Help for the 30/15 iPhone app and for 30/15 Coach on Android.

Delete your data is the way to remove one ride, every ride, or the account.

What the app is

30/15 is an interval timer and ride log for indoor cycling. It runs a session of hard efforts and easy recoveries, with spoken cues and an optional music bed. You can ride with nothing paired. If you connect a Bluetooth power meter or heart-rate sensor, the ride shows the numbers that sensor sends.

30/15 is free. Start does not ask you to create an account.

Get help

Open a GitHub issue and describe what you expected, what happened, and which iPhone and sensor you were using.

github.com/kaushikpavani/thirty-fifteen/issues

You can also write from the app: Settings → Leave a note. That note is stored for the developer to read. It does not require an account. The Privacy Policy describes what that note includes.

Pair a power meter or smart trainer

30/15 reads power over Bluetooth. It looks for the Cycling Power service. A smart trainer that does not offer that service can still connect through the Fitness Machine service. Discovery is by those services, not by brand name.

  1. Wake the meter (turn the cranks) or turn the trainer on, and turn Bluetooth on.
  2. On the home screen, tap the power tile. Or open Settings → Power meter.
  3. The first time you do this, iOS asks for Bluetooth. The prompt says 30/15 reads cycling power and heart rate from your sensors. The app does not ask for Bluetooth on first launch.
  4. When the meter appears under Nearby, tap it. The app remembers it and tries to reconnect later.

If nothing appears, another app or a bike computer may already be holding the Bluetooth connection. Close that link and search again. Meters that speak only ANT+ do not work on iPhone. A longer list of what can connect is in the app under What can I connect?

You can press Start with no meter. Live watts stay blank until a paired meter sends them.

Pair a heart-rate sensor

30/15 reads the Bluetooth Heart Rate service. That includes a chest strap and a watch that is broadcasting heart rate.

  1. Put the strap on, or turn on Bluetooth heart-rate broadcast on the watch. On many Garmin watches that is Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Heart Rate → Broadcast Heart Rate. Apple Watch does not broadcast on its own; a broadcast app on the watch has to be running.
  2. On the home screen, tap the heart-rate tile. Or open Settings → Heart rate.
  3. Tap the device under Nearby. Wake the watch if the row says it is waiting for a broadcast.

Live heart rate appears only when the sensor sends a beat. The ride still runs with no strap and no watch. More device notes are in the app under What can I connect?

No account, and offline

Home → Start does not ask you to sign in, and it does not wait for a network. The interval clock, spoken cues, and music bed run on the phone. Finished rides, FTP, and workout settings stay on the iPhone until you sign in.

Sign-in is optional. Settings → Account shows Continue with Apple and Continue with Google. After you sign in, finished rides and the settings described in the Privacy Policy can copy to your account when the phone is online.

Sound while the phone is locked

During a ride, spoken cues and the music bed are set to keep playing if you lock the iPhone or switch to another app. The iOS silent switch does not mute the ride. Bluetooth can keep delivering power and heart rate while the app is in the background. The clock does not need a network to keep time.

Delete your data

30/15 Coach is published by Kaushik Pavani (Sri Kaushik Pavani). On iPhone the same app’s home-screen name is 30/15. The steps below work in either app. You can delete one ride, or all of your rides, without deleting the account. You can also delete the account. If the app is not installed, use the request at the end of this section. You do not have to reinstall it to ask.

Delete one ride, and keep the account

  1. Open Settings → Past rides.
  2. Press and hold the ride, then confirm Delete ride. Or open the ride and tap Delete this ride.
  3. If you stop a ride before the sets are finished, choose Delete ride. That removes that ride the same way.

The ride leaves this phone immediately. If you are signed in, the cloud copy is removed the next time the app reaches the server. If it cannot, the delete stays recorded and is tried again. The account, the email address, and the user id stay.

Delete all rides, and keep the account

  1. Open Settings → Your data.
  2. Tap Delete ride history.
  3. Confirm Delete rides.

This removes the rides from this phone. If you are signed in, it also deletes the cloud copy of rides that had already ended. It does not delete the account, the email address, the user id, the profile, or feedback notes already stored with the account.

Delete other data on this phone, and keep the account

  1. Settings → Your data → Clear queued notes and analytics drops notes and product events that have not been sent yet. Notes already delivered stay until you delete the account, or stay unlinked if they were sent with no account.
  2. Settings → Your data → Erase data on this iPhone removes rides, settings, and saved sensors from this phone, and asks the backend to delete this install’s device row. On Android that row is Erase data on this phone. The account stays. Settings stored on the account can return the next time this phone syncs, unless you delete the account.

Reset to defaults, in the same Your data section, restores workout settings. It does not delete ride history.

Delete the account

  1. Sign in. On iPhone, Settings → Account shows Continue with Apple and Continue with Google. On Android, Continue with Apple is not shown, and Continue with Google is.
  2. Open Settings → Your data → Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm with Delete account to remove the cloud account and the cloud copy of your rides, profile, account notes, and account product events. Rides on this phone stay. On iPhone, confirm with Delete account and iPhone data to do that and also erase rides and settings on this iPhone. On Android the phone button says Delete account and phone data.

If the phone cannot reach the server, the account delete stays queued and is tried again. You are signed out after the server accepts it. There is no extra waiting period after that.

What is deleted

When the server accepts Delete account, 30/15 Coach deletes these from the account:

  • Email address, including an Apple private-relay address when that is the one on the account.
  • User id.
  • Display name on the profile.
  • Ride summaries: times, duration, FTP, target watts, completion, and any watts or heart-rate summary stored with the ride.
  • Fitness data stored on the account: FTP, age, sex, weight, resting heart rate, and the other workout settings copied with the account.
  • Feedback notes stored with the user id.
  • Product events stored with the user id.

The sign-in account itself is deleted. Install rows are unlinked from the account. The user id on those rows is cleared.

What is kept

  • Rides and settings on the phone, when you chose Delete account and did not also erase the phone. They stay until you delete them in the app or delete the app. If you create an account again later, rides still on the phone can be copied up again.
  • Product events and feedback notes sent with no user id. They are not linked to an account, so deleting an account does not select them. There is no timed purge of those rows.
  • A feedback note may already have been sent to the developer so it can be read. That mailbox copy is not removed by the in-app delete. The request below covers it.
  • This project does not keep its own archive of a deleted account, and it does not restore one. Android backup of the app is off, so a signed-out ride is not copied to a Google account by Android backup.

If you cannot open the app

Write to Kaushik Pavani on GitHub Issues. You do not need the app installed. github.com/kaushikpavani/thirty-fifteen/issues

  1. Open a new issue.
  2. Title it “Delete my 30/15 Coach account”.
  3. In the issue, include the email address on the account. Do not attach a ride file.

Kaushik Pavani deletes that account and the data listed under What is deleted, and deletes any mailbox copy of your feedback notes. Use the same issue if an in-app delete did not finish.
