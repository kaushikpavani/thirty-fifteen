Help for the 30/15 iPhone app.

What the app is

30/15 is an interval timer and ride log for indoor cycling. It runs a session of hard efforts and easy recoveries, with spoken cues and an optional music bed. You can ride with nothing paired. If you connect a Bluetooth power meter or heart-rate sensor, the ride shows the numbers that sensor sends.

30/15 is free. Start does not ask you to create an account.

Pair a power meter or smart trainer

30/15 reads power over Bluetooth. It looks for the Cycling Power service. A smart trainer that does not offer that service can still connect through the Fitness Machine service. Discovery is by those services, not by brand name.

  1. Wake the meter (turn the cranks) or turn the trainer on, and turn Bluetooth on.
  2. On the home screen, tap the power tile. Or open Settings → Power meter.
  3. The first time you do this, iOS asks for Bluetooth. The prompt says 30/15 reads cycling power and heart rate from your sensors. The app does not ask for Bluetooth on first launch.
  4. When the meter appears under Nearby, tap it. The app remembers it and tries to reconnect later.

If nothing appears, another app or a bike computer may already be holding the Bluetooth connection. Close that link and search again. Meters that speak only ANT+ do not work on iPhone. A longer list of what can connect is in the app under What can I connect?

You can press Start with no meter. Live watts stay blank until a paired meter sends them.

Pair a heart-rate sensor

30/15 reads the Bluetooth Heart Rate service. That includes a chest strap and a watch that is broadcasting heart rate.

  1. Put the strap on, or turn on Bluetooth heart-rate broadcast on the watch. On many Garmin watches that is Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Heart Rate → Broadcast Heart Rate. Apple Watch does not broadcast on its own; a broadcast app on the watch has to be running.
  2. On the home screen, tap the heart-rate tile. Or open Settings → Heart rate.
  3. Tap the device under Nearby. Wake the watch if the row says it is waiting for a broadcast.

Live heart rate appears only when the sensor sends a beat. The ride still runs with no strap and no watch. More device notes are in the app under What can I connect?

No account, and offline

Home → Start does not ask you to sign in, and it does not wait for a network. The interval clock, spoken cues, and music bed run on the phone. Finished rides, FTP, and workout settings stay on the iPhone until you sign in.

Sign-in is optional. Settings → Account shows Continue with Apple and Continue with Google. After you sign in, finished rides and the settings described in the Privacy Policy can copy to your account when the phone is online.

Sound while the phone is locked

During a ride, spoken cues and the music bed are set to keep playing if you lock the iPhone or switch to another app. The iOS silent switch does not mute the ride. Bluetooth can keep delivering power and heart rate while the app is in the background. The clock does not need a network to keep time.

Delete your data

These are the controls in the app.

  • One ride. Settings → Past rides, then press and hold the ride and confirm Delete ride. On the ride’s own page, tap Delete this ride. If you are signed in, that ride is also removed from the account. If you end a ride before the sets are finished, Delete ride does the same for that ride.
  • All rides. Settings → Your data → Delete ride history. This removes the rides from this iPhone. If you are signed in, it also deletes the cloud copy of rides that had already ended.
  • Notes and product events still on the phone. Settings → Your data → Clear queued notes and analytics. This drops notes and product events that have not been sent yet.
  • This iPhone. Settings → Your data → Erase data on this iPhone. This removes rides, settings, and saved sensors from this phone, and asks the backend to delete this install’s device row.
  • The account. Sign in, then Settings → Your data → Delete account and cloud data. Confirm with Delete account to remove the cloud account and the cloud copy of your rides, profile, account notes, and account product events. Rides on this iPhone stay. Confirm with Delete account and iPhone data to do that and also erase rides and settings on this iPhone. On Android the same row is Delete account and cloud data, and the phone button says Delete account and phone data. Continue with Apple is not shown on Android. If the phone cannot reach the server, the account delete stays queued and is tried again. You are signed out after the server accepts it.

Reset to defaults, in the same Your data section, restores workout settings. It does not delete ride history.
