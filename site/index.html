30/15

An iPhone interval coach for indoor cycling.

What it does

30/15 times a session of hard efforts and easy recoveries, speaks the cues, and can play a music bed under the ride. A Bluetooth power meter or heart-rate sensor is optional. Live watts and live heart rate appear only when a sensor you pair is sending them.

You can start with no account and no network. Finished rides stay on the iPhone until you choose to sign in. Sign-in is in Settings, with Apple or Google. 30/15 is free.
